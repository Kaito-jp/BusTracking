Account & Data Deletion Policy

App Name: PiliBus (BusTracking)

Developer: Pagadian Bus Tracker

At PiliBus, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal information and account data.

1. How to Delete Your Account and Associated Data

In-App Deletion:

  1. Open the PiliBus app on your Android device.
  2. Log in and navigate to your Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down to ACCOUNT ACTION and tap Delete My Data.
  4. Confirm deletion. This permanently removes all your personal database records and deletes your Firebase Authentication account.

2. Requesting Data Deletion Without Deleting Your Account

You can contact our support team to request removal of specific records while keeping your account active.
